# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

US business owners and marketing decision-makers evaluating an experienced search, paid media, and web partner. They arrive needing credible help with visibility, acquisition, or a site that performs, and want direct access to the person responsible for the work.

## Product Purpose

Advanced Digital Marketing LTDA provides SEO, GEO, paid search, paid social, and web design services. The public site should make the offer understandable quickly and route qualified prospects to a direct project inquiry.

## Positioning

One senior, owner-operated practitioner handles strategy, implementation, and measurement rather than handing work through an account-management layer. The business applies US-market playbooks from a Brazilian LTDA and serves companies operating across the US and Brazil.

## Operating Context

Prospects evaluate the agency from a desktop or mobile browser, often comparing it with generalist agencies. Contact happens through a direct email link, not an intake form. The registered office is in São Paulo, Brazil.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Public pages are Home, About, and Contact, built with SvelteKit and deployed as prerendered web pages. Preserve the existing routes, direct email behavior, registered-office details, services, and accessibility behavior. Do not fabricate results, client logos, testimonials, certifications, partnerships, or performance claims.

## Brand Commitments

The site should feel vivid, polished, and trustworthy within a Japanese cyberpunk world. Deep blue surfaces and vermillion red are binding; pure black, cyan, and magenta are not. Japanese text and background kanji must be live markup, the desktop vertical Japanese rail must remain fixed while the visitor scrolls, and `new-hero.png` must not be rendered as a flattened hero. The supplied city photo should lead the site, and About uses only `andrew-portrait-v3-full.png` as Andrew's portrait.

- Supplied assets: `new-assets/night-city.png`, `new-assets/city-ink.jpg`, `new-assets/andrew-portrait-v3-full.png`, Japanese font subsets, and logo artwork.

## Product Principles

- Make the owner-operated difference concrete.
- Lead with search visibility while keeping the complete service offering legible.
- Make the direct next step unmistakable.
- Use expressive art direction without sacrificing comprehension or access.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Respect reduced-motion preferences, preserve visible keyboard focus and semantic controls, maintain readable contrast over imagery, and keep the full experience usable on narrow mobile screens.
